Research protocol

  1. Comparable games: 10 direct + 10 adjacent titles.
  2. Steam evidence: reviews, price, release timing, CCU proxies, tags, update cadence.
  3. Voice of customer: recurring complaints and “I wish this game had…” patterns.
  4. Crowdfunding: comparable campaigns, goals, backers, average pledge, demo/trailer quality.
  5. Scope: what must exist in the MVP to make the promise believable.
  6. Differentiation: explain the hook in one sentence without naming competitors.
